# Critic Lens: Advertising, Marketing, Copy, and Growth

## When to Use

This lens is a checklist every critic carries: in a Critic family run, all five full-spectrum critics load it alongside `persona-skeptic` and work it in full, on every artifact, whatever the artifact type. On artifacts of its shape (ad creative, landing copy, a positioning statement, a pricing page, an email, a launch plan, or a growth experiment), this lens's findings lead the merged report. On an artifact with no marketing surface, every critic works this checklist against what exists and reports the surface absent. This lens separates what the maker likes from what actually works, and hunts the claims that will get David sued or refunded.

## The first question: does it fit the brief

Before taste, check fit. A beautiful ad that misses the brief is a failure.
- Does the key message and tone match the brief's objective and audience.
- Is the takeaway singular. One ad, one idea. If a reader cannot repeat the offer back in one sentence, it failed (clarity beats cleverness every time).

## Substantiation and legal risk (the money finding)

- Every claim on a paid or public surface must be substantiable. "Proven", "guaranteed", "best", "most", "free" have legal definitions and evidentiary bars.
- Unsubstantiated comparative and superiority claims are FTC risk (US) and a refund and chargeback risk. Flag each as OVERSTATED or UNBACKED with the substantiation that would clear it.
- Testimonials and results carry disclosure requirements. "Results not typical" is not a fix for an atypical claim.

## Positioning and UVP attack
- **Clarity over cleverness.** If the value proposition needs a second read, cut it.
- **The one-sentence test.** Can a reader explain the offer to a friend after one read.
- **Differentiation.** What does this say that the competitor cannot say truthfully. If the copy would work with the competitor's logo swapped, it is generic.
- **Audience specificity.** "Everyone" is no one. Name the ICP and the job to be done.

## Performance-creative reality check
- Hook in the first 3 seconds or first line, or the attention is gone.
- One CTA, one intent. Multiple competing CTAs convert none.
- Does the creative promise match the destination. Bait headline plus mismatched landing page is a conversion killer and a trust breach.
- Format fit: does the creative match the placement (feed vs story vs search intent vs email subject).

## What the growth skeptic always demands

- **The mechanism.** Why will this convert. "It looks good" is not a mechanism. Name the psychological or economic driver.
- **The number's source.** Projected revenue, CAC, or lift must trace to a model or a real cohort, not optimism. When numbers get heavy, work the claims checklist in full as well; every critic carries it, so this is a deeper pass over the same brief, not a handoff.
- **The honest comparison.** Against the top competitor the customer would actually weigh, not a strawman.

## Output additions (append to the core contract)
- **Substantiation ledger.** Every claim, its verdict label, the evidence bar it must clear.
- **Brief-fit score.** Scored against the brief on three components, 0 to 3 each: objective fit, audience fit, and single-takeaway fit. Passing is a total of 6 of 9 or better with no component at 0. Report the three component scores, not just the total (checklist item GROWTH-BRF-001).
- **The swap test.** Would this work under a competitor's logo. Yes means generic.

## Mandatory marketing-surface checks (owned by this lens)

Checklist items GROWTH-MMS-001 (ad pixels and conversion tracking) and GROWTH-MMS-002 (local business listing and social presence) are owned by this lens. The UX lens carries the same two checks for website critiques, with the procedures inlined there as well, because a website verdict on an unmeasured surface is incomplete from either side. Run them from whichever lens reaches the surface first. The pixel audit: inventory every pixel and tag, fire each conversion event and confirm its pixel, confirm server-side CAPI events deduplicate against browser events on the shared event id, and confirm no pixel fires before consent. The listing audit's canonical steps are checklist item GROWTH-MMS-002. An unperformable check is recorded Not tested with the reason, never silently skipped.

## Failure modes this lens exists to catch

- Maker-taste review, not audience review. "I like it" is not a finding.
- Clever headline that fails the one-sentence test.
- Unsubstantiated superiority claim waiting to be a legal problem.
- Creative-to-landing mismatch. Promise the thing the page delivers.
- Growth number with no cohort or model behind it.

## Refinement pass additions (P-18)

- **Barriers scan (CS-14).** For product-shaped artifacts, scan for ship-readiness barriers beyond findings: unapproved integrations, missing certifications, platform rules, license problems. Each barrier is a record (category, what it blocks, severity, evidence); a blocks-launch barrier caps the verdict at fix-first regardless of finding counts.
- **Substantiation as parity.** Load-bearing marketing claims join the claims parity table with an evidence ID or an UNBACKED label; a growth number with no cohort or model behind it is UNBACKED, not a projection.
- **Not tested.** This lens's return ends with a Not tested list.
