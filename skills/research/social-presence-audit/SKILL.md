# Social Presence Audit: listings, Google Business Profile, consistency

## When to Use

For a local service business (Patriot Pest Control, and any AlphaFlux whitelabel customer) before or during a marketing push. Checks whether the business actually shows up, is complete, and is kept current across the places customers look. Pairs with `ad-tracking-pixels` (tracking) and `seo-reference` (site).

## What to audit, platform by platform

### Google Business Profile (the listing that matters most locally)

This is the single most important local surface, and David specifically asked what a Google listing should be and how to keep it current.

- [ ] **Claimed and verified** the profile.
- [ ] **Primary category** matches what the business actually does (e.g. Pest Control), plus accurate secondary categories. Categories are a top ranking lever.
- [ ] **NAP consistency:** Name, Address, Phone identical to the website and every other listing (a mismatch hurts local ranking and trust).
- [ ] **Hours** current, including holidays and special hours.
- [ ] **Description** written to what the business does now (update when services change).
- [ ] **Photos/video** present and recent (storefront, team, job photos).
- [ ] **Services and pricing** listed if applicable.
- [ ] **Posts/updates** used occasionally (Google refreshes signals; stale profiles look abandoned).
- [ ] **Q&A** monitored (owner can seed and answer; unanswered public Q&A looks bad).
- [ ] **Reviews:** quantity, rating, and whether the owner RESPONDS to every review (response rate is a trust and ranking signal).
- [ ] **Keep it current:** when services, hours, service area, or ownership change, update the profile that same week. A profile describing the wrong services is worse than none.

### Social platforms (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, others)

For each the business should reasonably be on:
- [ ] **Presence:** does an account exist, is it claimed (watch for impersonators on Facebook especially).
- [ ] **Handle/brand consistency** across platforms (same name and @handle where possible).
- [ ] **NAP consistency** in bios (matches Google listing and site exactly).
- [ ] **Profile complete:** bio, link to site, category, contact, profile/cover image.
- [ ] **Link back to the website** (and the right landing page, not just home).
- [ ] **Activity/refresh:** how stale is the last post (a dead account signals an abandoned business).
- [ ] **Cross-linking:** platforms link to each other and to the site.

### The consistency pass (the part most audits miss)

- [ ] **NAP identical everywhere:** one typo'd phone or old address across listings splits the business's local presence and confuses both customers and search engines.
- [ ] **Categories/descriptions consistent** with what the business does TODAY (not what it did 2 years ago).
- [ ] **No dead or claimed-wrong listings** (old addresses, old numbers, duplicate Facebook pages).
- [ ] **Reviews aggregated:** is reputation consistent across Google/Facebook, or does one channel have a problem showing.

## Output

A per-platform table: platform | present? | complete? | NAP match? | current? | issue + fix. Then a prioritized fix list (Google Business Profile issues first, then broken/inconsistent listings, then staleness). Every finding has the observable evidence (what the listing actually shows vs what it should).

## Why this matters for David

- **Patriot Pest Control:** local customers find it via Google listing + Facebook. An incomplete or stale listing loses calls directly. David suspected a prior researcher ("Lulu") had guidance on Google listing upkeep; this skill encodes the standing best practice (claimed, correct category, current hours/services, reviews responded to, refreshed) so it does not depend on a specific doc.
- **AlphaFlux:** whitelabel customers are local service businesses; a social/listing audit is a repeatable onboarding + ongoing check the platform can productize.

## Rules

- Report what the listing ACTUALLY shows (screenshot or quoted text) vs what it should be; never assume a profile is complete.
- Verify against the live listing, not memory. Use `deep-research` / browser checks where access allows.
- Google Business Profile and platform policies change; re-verify ranking/review specifics against current Google guidance before advising on them.
