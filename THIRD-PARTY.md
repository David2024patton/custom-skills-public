# Third-Party Skills and Licensing

This file is the licensing map for this repo. It was generated by a frontmatter
sweep of every SKILL.md in the tree (python3, standard library only) on
2026-10-26, and the counts below are the sweep's counts, not estimates.

Sweep totals: 281 SKILL.md files. 157 state license MIT in their frontmatter.
124 state no license at all. 45 name David Patton as author.

What a consumer may do with this repo:

- David Patton's own work (section B) is covered by the MIT license in the
  root LICENSE file.
- Third party skills that claim MIT (section A) may be redistributed under
  the MIT terms, with the copyright and permission notice kept. Each of
  those folders now carries its own LICENSE file naming its author.
- Skills that state no license (section C) are all rights reserved by
  default. They are NOT cleared for redistribution and are excluded from
  any handover, mirror, or MCP packaging until the author's grant is on
  file. Presence in this repo is not permission.

Note on the Go skills: the `golang-*` folders are upstream snapshots of
samber/cc-skills-golang, and the `local-golang-*` folders are David's local
working variants of the same skills. Both sets are kept on purpose; the
folder name is the identity, and the two sets are not interchangeable.
